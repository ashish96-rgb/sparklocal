# SparkLocal Demo Video Script

**Target length:** 2-3 minutes. Record the app in a browser with readable zoom and no personal data. For an authentic Gemma demo, configure and verify a Gemma provider before recording. The app labels offline output as demo mode; do not present that output as a live model response.

## 0:00-0:20 | The problem

**Show:** SparkLocal home and the community board.

**Say:** “A lot of community projects begin with someone noticing a local problem. The hard part is turning that concern into a first step, finding people to help, and learning whether it made a difference.”

## 0:20-0:45 | Introduce the idea

**Show:** The project form and Gemma model status.

**Say:** “SparkLocal uses open-weight Gemma to help a community shape a practical project brief. We give it the challenge, time, resources, and the people who might take part. The AI drafts a plan; the community stays in charge.”

## 0:45-1:25 | Create a plan

**Action:** Enter a non-sensitive example, such as a lack of shade at a neighborhood bus stop. Set a location, timeline, budget, and participating group. Submit and show steps, roles, supplies, impact measures, and risks.

**Say:** “The plan is structured for action: what to try first, who might help, what it could require, what to measure, and what could get in the way. We avoid asking for personal data, and the model is clearly identified.”

## 1:25-1:55 | Make it collaborative

**Action:** Add the brief to the board. Search or filter. Join and support a community brief.

**Say:** “A plan does not have to stop with the person who wrote it. We can share the brief, see participation across browsers, and make the next step visible.”

## 1:55-2:20 | Open innovation

**Show:** README Gemma setup and contribution guide.

**Say:** “Open innovation matters because the useful knowledge is distributed. Communities can inspect and adapt this project, run Gemma on infrastructure they control, and contribute local experience and improvements. Open weights add choice; they do not replace consent or human judgment.”

## 2:20-2:35 | Close

**Show:** The project name and repository/live links once created.

**Say:** “SparkLocal turns a local concern into an open invitation to act. Start small, share what works, and let neighbors shape what comes next.”
