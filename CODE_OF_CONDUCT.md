# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in SparkLocal to be welcoming and constructive for people of every background and level of experience. We value respectful disagreement, curiosity, and collaboration across differences.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Focus feedback on ideas and work, not personal traits.
- Welcome questions and make room for community knowledge.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing another person's story or information.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes when they cause harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted personal contact, publishing private information without consent, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers using the contact method listed on the GitHub repository. Include relevant links or context without redistributing sensitive information. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter privacy where possible, and take proportionate action, including editing or removing content, issuing warnings, or restricting participation. This code applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
